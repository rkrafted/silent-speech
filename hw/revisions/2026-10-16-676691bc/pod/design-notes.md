# ss-pod v1 schematic: design notes (rev 0.4)

Rev 0.2 is Claude's rebuild of the DeepSeek draft (rev 0.1, commit d74f075). Every pin and value
below was checked against the datasheets in `hw/pod/ref/` (gitignored). Items still waiting on a
datasheet are marked **OPEN**. The schematic is generated by `gen_sch.py`. Edit the generator and
not the `.kicad_sch` file, because the next run overwrites manual edits.

Verification, all re-run after every change:

```
python3 hw/pod/gen_sch.py
kicad-cli sch erc --severity-error --exit-code-violations -o hw/pod/erc.rpt hw/pod/pod.kicad_sch   # 0 errors
kicad-cli sch export netlist -o hw/pod/pod.net hw/pod/pod.kicad_sch
python3 hw/pod/check_nets.py hw/pod/pod.net                                                       # 122 rules
```

ERC leaves 6 warnings, and all of them are intended. TESTP_PACE_OUT1/2 are tied to AVDD, and GPIO1..4
are tied to GND, exactly as TI specifies. KiCad warns because those pins are "bidirectional".
`check_nets.py` was itself tested against rev 0.1: it flags every bug listed in section 2.

## 1. What it is

A battery-powered 8-channel surface-EMG front end for a small wearable sensor pod. It is built from these parts:

- **Analog front end:** an ADS1298IPAG (TQFP-64) on a unipolar 3.3 V supply. All 8 channels are
  referential: IN1P..IN8P go to electrodes E1..E8, and IN1N..IN8N go to the REF electrode. One bias
  electrode is driven by the chip's RLD amplifier.
- **Radio:** a u-blox **NINA-B306-00B** module (nRF52840, 10 × 15 mm, internal antenna, 32.768 kHz
  crystal on board, CE/RED). It talks to the ADS1298 over SPI and streams over BLE.
- **Battery input:** one protected 1-cell LiPo, through an **AO3401A reverse-polarity FET**.
- **Charging:** an **MCP73831** at 100 mA from the pogo VBUS pin.
- **Supplies:** two **TPS7A2033** low-noise regulators (7 µV RMS, pin-compatible with the LP5907, which is out of stock). LDO1 feeds AVDD only, and LDO2 feeds DVDD and the
  module.
- **Electrode lines:** each of the 10 lines has an ESD array, a 2.2 k series resistor and a 1 nF
  capacitor to ground (OpenBCI Cyton values).
- **Connector:** 12 individual SMD spring pins (**Harwin P70-1000045R**) carry E1..E8, REF, BIAS, VBUS and GND. They sit on the same (top) side as every other part, in a 2 × 6 grid at 3.0 mm pitch at one board end. P11 (VBUS) and P12 (GND) are adjacent, so the charger tab touches only those two.

## 2a. Rev 0.3 changes (2026-10-12, from the MobilECG-II comparison in `ref-compare.md`)

1. **BIAS series 10 k (R25)**: RLDOUT → R25 10 k → C10 1 nF + bead → BIAS pad. The RLD amplifier no longer
   drives C10, the ESD capacitance and the strip directly (series R isolates a capacitive load), and fault
   current into skin is limited. RLD feedback (R10 ‖ C11) is taken at RLDOUT, before R25. Answers reviewer
   question 2 (section 8). MobilECG-II uses the same 10 k.
2. **33 Ω source series resistors (R26–R30)** on SCLK, MOSI, CS_N (NINA drives) and MISO, DRDY (ADS drives).
   Nets: NINA side keeps the old name, ADS side is `<name>_A`. CS pull-up R21 is on CS_N_A.
3. **Ferrite bead per electrode line (FB1–FB10)** between the ESD array and the series R (BIAS: between pad
   and C10). Against BLE-burst pickup on the strip. MobilECG-II and the 12-lead holter use the same pattern.

**OPEN (parts):** LCSC numbers for the 33 Ω 0402 (Yageo RC0402FR-0733RL or the JLCPCB basic equivalent) and
for BLM15HD182SN1D, plus its impedance at 2.4 GHz (want ≥ 1 kΩ; otherwise pick a BLM15GG/HG type).

## 2b. Rev 0.4 changes (2026-10-14, from the layout)

1. **ADS1298 link moved to the NINA's outer left pin column.** The inner-ring pins (31-46) cannot be
   escaped on this LGA: the outer pads are 0.3 mm apart. New map, in the ADS1298's pin order:
   MOSI = pin 8 (P1.00), PWDN = 7 (P0.25), RESET = 5 (P0.24), START = 4 (P0.16), CS = 3 (P0.15),
   SCLK = 2 (P0.14), MISO = 1 (P0.13), DRDY = 25 (P0.04). UBX-17052099 flags **none of these as near the
   radio**, which is better than rev 0.3: P1.01-P1.11 were near the radio.
2. **SPI 33 ohm series resistors R26-R30 removed.** The ADS1298 sits under that pin column, so each line
   is about 3 mm (pad -> via -> pad). That is electrically short for nRF52840 standard-drive edges.
3. **EXP1-EXP5 (pins 17, 18, 20, 21, 22) ARE near the radio** (UBX-17052099): use them only for signals
   below 10 kHz, so no 100/400 kHz I2C there. BAT_SENSE (pin 16, P0.03) is near the radio too; it is a
   slow analog input, which is fine.
4. **SWD on a Tag-Connect TC2030-NL footprint J3** (pads only; cable TC2030-CTX-NL) instead of loose test
   pads. Test and expansion pads are 0.8 mm.
5. **6-layer board** (see section 6).

## 2c. Rev 0.5 changes (2026-10-16, owner review of the 3D model)

1. **Battery-sled contacts TP15 (BATT+, on BAT_RAW) and TP16 (BATT-, GND)**: two 2.5 mm ENIG pads on the bottom
   face, in parallel with the JST socket J2. A v2 snap-in battery sled with its own spring contacts can then power
   the pod without tools (owner decision: modular, swappable cells). Placed on the two largest free spots of the
   bottom face, 16 mm apart, far from the antenna end: TP15 at (105.2, 107.0) under the Tag-Connect footprint
   (TC2030-NL has no legs, so nothing comes through), TP16 at (109.0, 122.9). The sled's + goes through the AO3401A
   like the plug, so a reversed sled is blocked too. **Use one cell at a time (plug or sled), never both.** A swap
   without a hold-up cap restarts the pod (about 1 s plus a BLE reconnect); the flash buffer covers it.
2. **Electrode tab without overhang**: the right-angle 1x10 header that stuck out past the pod edge is replaced by
   an upright SMD 2x5 2.54 mm header on the tab's top face. The tab is L-shaped and stays inside the pod outline:
   the grid part (x 100-110.4) plus a foot over the pod's low parts (all <= 1.25 mm; the tab rides 3.0 mm up) that
   stops 0.5 mm short of the JST socket and clear of the antenna end. All tab traces are on F.Cu (the pod side
   carries only the contacts), so the header pin order is whatever routes planar; `route_tab.py` finds it and the
   silkscreen prints it.
3. **Charger tab unchanged**: inside the outline its USB-C shell legs would land on C28 or the JST socket. It is only
   attached while charging, so it keeps its overhang.

## 5. Firmware rules the hardware depends on

- **Power-up:** drive PWDN and RESET high, wait for t_POR, pulse RESET, send SDATAC, and only then
  write registers (SBAS459K power-up sequence).
- **ADS1298 registers:**
  - CONFIG3: `PD_REFBUF = 1` (internal reference), `RLDREF_INT = 1`, `PD_RLD = 1`.
  - RLD_SENSP/N: select the electrode channels.
  - Gain: x6 or lower for EMG (the forum found x24 rails).
  - Data rate: 500–1000 SPS.
  - Do not use the pace/test outputs. They are tied to AVDD.
- **Charging:** on USBDETECTED (charger present), stop streaming and power the ADS down. On
  USBREMOVED, resume. Report "charging" to the app while VBUS is present, and "full" once the battery
  reads ≥ 4.15 V for a few minutes (STAT is not wired: it swings to 5 V).
- **No light anywhere:** there are no LEDs. Every status (connected, recording, battery, charging)
  goes to the phone app over BLE.
- **Battery:** read BAT_SENSE × 2. Below 3.5 V, warn and stop, because LDO dropout starts near 3.4 V.
- **Idle:** keep PWDN low and advertise BLE slowly. There is no power switch, so idle current sets
  shelf life (tens of µA means months on 200 mAh).

## 6. Layout (rev 0.4 board, 2026-10-14)

Rebuild everything with `hw/pod/build.sh`:

| Step | Script | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `gen_sch.py` | schematic, ERC, netlist, `check_nets.py` |
| 2 | `gen_nina_fp.py` | module footprint |
| 3 | `gen_pcb.py` | placement, rules, planes, keep-outs, locked decoupling routes, GND fan-out |
| 4 | `route.py` | Freerouting |
| 5 | `finish_route.py` | A* for leftovers, GND stitching, clearance nudges |
| 6 | DRC | must be clean |

Order files come from `fab.py`, which refuses on any DRC error, unrouted net or placeholder. The mating
tabs come from `gen_tabs.py`, and the electrode tab is then routed by `route_tab.py` (F.Cu only). Shared mechanics are in `pod_geom.py`. The review package is `hw/review/`.

- **Board:** 24 x 30 mm, **6 layers**: F.Cu / In1 GND / In2 sig / In3 sig / In4 GND / B.Cu, parts on both
  sides. The owner chose 24 x 30 after 22 x 28 measured 76 % courtyard density (893 mm2 of parts). Six
  layers instead of four (about $10-30 more per order) because:
  - on four layers the autorouter left 8-27 nets open in every attempt;
  - an In2 +3V3 plane split into islands;
  - six layers give each assembled side its own solid ground plane.
  The 150 mAh pouch (19.75 x 26.0 x 3.8) sits against the bottom side.
- **Top:**
  - NINA-B306 at the top edge, antenna outward (footprint rotated 180 deg), with an all-layer copper
    keep-out under its antenna end;
  - 3 x 4 pogo grid (3.0 mm pitch, P1-P3 / P4-P6 / P7-P9 / P10-P12, P11 VBUS + P12 GND adjacent), with
    an M2 NPTH clamp hole at each end;
  - Tag-Connect between H1 and the grid;
  - VBUS TVS under P11;
  - flash directly under the module's QSPI pin row;
  - charger, FET, JST SH socket (opening at the bottom edge);
  - the boot pull resistors and battery divider in two columns at the right edge.
- **Bottom:**
  - ADS1298 under the module;
  - every decoupling cap 2 mm outside its own pin, with a **locked** pad-to-pin route;
  - VREFP 10 uF and VCAP1 22 uF (0805) in a second row, lined up on free gaps;
  - the 10 filter rows (bead, 2.2k, 1 nF) under the pogo grid in ADS pin order, ESD arrays in the
    grid-area corners;
  - both LDOs, with EN tied to IN on a locked route;
  - 0.8 mm test and expansion pads.
- **Clamp:** the electrode tab and the charger tab screw down on 3.0 mm M2 nylon spacers (pogo
  working height 2.9 mm). Hole keep-out is r 2.1 mm on both sides (spacer on top, screw head below).
- **Tabs:**
  - the pod-facing side carries only the contact pads, so a pogo tip can't press through the mask onto
    other copper;
  - the charger tab's USB-C (through-hole shell) sits in an overhang left of the pod outline; the electrode tab
    stays inside it (rev 0.5);
  - electrode tab header (rev 0.5, upright SMD 2x5, pin 1 top left, odd pins left column): 1 REF, 2 E4, 3 E2,
    4 E5, 5 E8, 6 E1, 7 BIAS, 8 E7, 9 E3, 10 E6. The order is the one that routes on the top layer alone; the
    silkscreen prints it beside the header.
- **Rules (JLCPCB 6-layer standard):**
  - signals routed at 0.10 / 0.10 mm; necked tracks widened to the 0.09 mm minimum;
  - DRC uses 0.09 mm space;
  - vias 0.45 / 0.20 mm;
  - via hole to copper 0.20 mm (confirm in review);
  - copper to edge 0.30 mm;
  - charge path (VBUS, +BATT, BAT_RAW) 0.20 mm tracks.
- **Ground:** every GND SMD pad gets its own stub and via where one fits. The pads listed in the review
  README reach ground through the pour plus stitching vias, and DRC confirms them connected.

## 8. Questions for the reviewers (TI E2E, r/PrintedCircuitBoard)

1. **RLD loop stability.** The bias electrode will often be a dry or hydrogel pad with 0.1–2 MΩ
   contact impedance that varies. Is TI's 392 k ‖ 10 nF still stable, or should C11 change?
2. **Series resistance on BIAS.** *Answered in rev 0.3:* 10 k (R25), as on MobilECG-II.
3. **Internal RLDREF** on a unipolar 3.3 V build: any reason to prefer an external mid-supply divider?
4. **MCP73831 without power-path.** The pod draws about 5 mA while the cell charges at 100 mA. Do we
   accept the termination behaviour for v1?
5. **Single GND net** with a split-return layout under the ADS1298, versus separate AGND/DGND nets
   joined at one point.
6. **VCAP1 22 µF X5R in a wearable** (TI notes microphonics under vibration). Is it worth a
   tantalum/polymer part?
