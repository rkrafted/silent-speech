# ss-pod v1 schematic: design notes (rev 0.8)

Rev 0.2 is Claude's rebuild of the DeepSeek draft (rev 0.1, commit d74f075). Every pin and value
below was checked against the datasheets in `hw/pod/ref/` (gitignored). Items still waiting on a
datasheet are marked **OPEN**. The schematic is generated by `gen_sch.py`. Edit the generator and
not the `.kicad_sch` file, because the next run overwrites manual edits.

Verification, all re-run after every change:

```
python3 hw/pod/gen_sch.py
kicad-cli sch erc --severity-error --exit-code-violations -o hw/pod/erc.rpt hw/pod/pod.kicad_sch   # 0 errors
kicad-cli sch export netlist -o hw/pod/pod.net hw/pod/pod.kicad_sch
python3 hw/pod/check_nets.py hw/pod/pod.net                                                       # 129 rules
```

ERC leaves 6 warnings, and all of them are intended. TESTP_PACE_OUT1/2 are tied to AVDD, and GPIO1..4
are tied to GND, exactly as TI specifies. KiCad warns because those pins are "bidirectional".
`check_nets.py` was itself tested against rev 0.1: it flags every bug listed in section 2.

## 1. What it is

A battery-powered 8-channel surface-EMG front end for a small wearable sensor pod. It is built from these parts:

- **Analog front end:** an ADS1298IPAG (TQFP-64) on a unipolar 3.3 V supply. All 8 channels are
  referential: IN1P..IN8P go to electrodes E1..E8, and IN1N..IN8N go to the REF electrode. One bias
  electrode is driven by the chip's RLD amplifier.
- **Radio:** a u-blox **NINA-B306-00B** module (nRF52840, 10 × 15 mm, internal antenna, 32.768 kHz
  crystal on board, CE/RED). It talks to the ADS1298 over SPI and streams over BLE.
- **Battery input:** one protected 1-cell LiPo on the swappable cartridge (or the bench JST), through an
  **AO3401A reverse-polarity FET**.
- **Charging (rev 0.7):** none on the pod. The cartridge is charged on the separate dock board (`hw/dock`).
- **Fuel gauge (rev 0.7):** MAX17048 on +BATT, I2C on EXP4/EXP5.
- **Supplies:** two **TPS7A2033** low-noise regulators (7 µV RMS, pin-compatible with the LP5907, which is out of stock). LDO1 feeds AVDD only, and LDO2 feeds DVDD and the
  module.
- **Electrode lines:** each of the 10 lines has an ESD array, a 2.2 k series resistor and a 1 nF
  capacitor to ground (OpenBCI Cyton values).
- **Connector:** 12 individual SMD spring pins (**Harwin P70-1000045R**) carry E1..E8, REF, BIAS, CLK_POGO and GND,
  in a 3 × 4 grid at 3.0 mm pitch on the top side. Rev 0.7: P11 = CLK_POGO (the ADS clock, for linking two pods;
  it was VBUS) and P12 = GND (the link's shared ground).

## 2a. Rev 0.3 changes (2026-10-12, from the MobilECG-II comparison in `ref-compare.md`)

1. **BIAS series 10 k (R25)**: RLDOUT → R25 10 k → C10 1 nF + bead → BIAS pad. The RLD amplifier no longer
   drives C10, the ESD capacitance and the strip directly (series R isolates a capacitive load), and fault
   current into skin is limited. RLD feedback (R10 ‖ C11) is taken at RLDOUT, before R25. Answers reviewer
   question 2 (section 8). MobilECG-II uses the same 10 k.
2. **33 Ω source series resistors (R26–R30)** on SCLK, MOSI, CS_N (NINA drives) and MISO, DRDY (ADS drives).
   Nets: NINA side keeps the old name, ADS side is `<name>_A`. CS pull-up R21 is on CS_N_A.
3. **Ferrite bead per electrode line (FB1–FB10)** between the ESD array and the series R (BIAS: between pad
   and C10). Against BLE-burst pickup on the strip. MobilECG-II and the 12-lead holter use the same pattern.

**OPEN (parts):** LCSC numbers for the 33 Ω 0402 (Yageo RC0402FR-0733RL or the JLCPCB basic equivalent) and
for BLM15HD182SN1D, plus its impedance at 2.4 GHz (want ≥ 1 kΩ; otherwise pick a BLM15GG/HG type).

## 2b. Rev 0.4 changes (2026-10-14, from the layout)

1. **ADS1298 link moved to the NINA's outer left pin column.** The inner-ring pins (31-46) cannot be
   escaped on this LGA: the outer pads are 0.3 mm apart. New map, in the ADS1298's pin order:
   MOSI = pin 8 (P1.00), PWDN = 7 (P0.25), RESET = 5 (P0.24), START = 4 (P0.16), CS = 3 (P0.15),
   SCLK = 2 (P0.14), MISO = 1 (P0.13), DRDY = 25 (P0.04). UBX-17052099 flags **none of these as near the
   radio**, which is better than rev 0.3: P1.01-P1.11 were near the radio.
2. **SPI 33 ohm series resistors R26-R30 removed.** The ADS1298 sits under that pin column, so each line
   is about 3 mm (pad -> via -> pad). That is electrically short for nRF52840 standard-drive edges.
3. **EXP1-EXP5 (pins 17, 18, 20, 21, 22) ARE near the radio** (UBX-17052099): use them only for signals
   below 10 kHz, so no 100/400 kHz I2C there. BAT_SENSE (pin 16, P0.03) is near the radio too; it is a
   slow analog input, which is fine.
4. **SWD on a Tag-Connect TC2030-NL footprint J3** (pads only; cable TC2030-CTX-NL) instead of loose test
   pads. Test and expansion pads are 0.8 mm.
5. **6-layer board** (see section 6).

## 2c. Rev 0.5 / 0.6 changes (2026-10-16, owner review of the 3D model)

1. **Swappable battery cartridge (rev 0.6; owner decision: modular, swappable cells).** The cell no longer lives
   inside the pod. It sits on a removable cartridge (`hw/cartridge/`, generated by `gen_cartridge.py`): a 0.6 mm
   2-layer board that forms the pod's bottom, with a JST SH socket J1 for the cell's plug and two tall SMD pogo pins
   (Harwin P70-1100045R, C20826701: 6.3 mm free, 5.0 mm working, 0.4 um gold; picked 2026-10-17) that press up onto
   two 1.8 mm ENIG pads on the pod's bottom face:
   TP15 = BATT+ on BAT_RAW at (110.9, 126.1) and TP16 = BATT- on GND at (112.4, 128.6). Swap = unclip one cartridge,
   clip in a charged one: no tools, no soldering. Spare cartridges can be sold separately.
   - **Cell:** 20 x 20 x 4 mm pouch (402020 class, about 120 mAh, about 17-20 h at 5-6 mA), at y 104.8-124.8, clear
     of the H1 screw head. It leaves the strip y 125-130 for the pads, the pins and J1. Footprint unchanged; the
     0.6 mm cartridge board replaces the 0.8 mm printed bottom wall, so the thickness is unchanged too.
   - **Pads diagonal, 2.9 mm apart:** a misplaced pin can never bridge + and -, which would short the cell.
   - **Test/expansion pads** moved from that strip to the free spot by H1 (3 x 3 at 1.3 mm around (110.4, 103.4)).
     They stay reachable: the cartridge comes off.
   - The cartridge's + also goes through the AO3401A, so a reversed cell is blocked like a reversed plug.
     J1 pin 1 = +, the same as the pod's own J2. **One cell at a time:** J2 (bench) or the cartridge, never both.
   - A swap restarts the pod (about 1 s plus a BLE reconnect); the flash buffer covers it.
   - The clip and the locating features that hold the cartridge to within about 0.4 mm belong to the case.
2. **Electrode tab without overhang**: the right-angle 1x10 header that stuck out past the pod edge is replaced by
   an upright SMD 2x5 2.54 mm header on the tab's top face. The tab is L-shaped and stays inside the pod outline:
   the grid part (x 100-110.4) plus a foot over the pod's low parts (all <= 1.25 mm; the tab rides 3.0 mm up) that
   stops 0.5 mm short of the JST socket and clear of the antenna end. All tab traces are on F.Cu (the pod side
   carries only the contacts), so the header pin order is whatever routes planar; `route_tab.py` finds it and the
   silkscreen prints it.
3. **Charger tab unchanged**: inside the outline its USB-C shell legs would land on C28 or the JST socket. It is only
   attached while charging, so it keeps its overhang.

## 2d. Rev 0.7 changes (2026-10-16, owner decisions: no charging on the pod, two pods worn at once)

1. **No USB charging on the pod.** U5 MCP73831, R11, C28, D5 SMAJ5.0A, R16 and the VBUS net are removed, and so is
   the charger tab. Cells are charged in their cartridge on the new **dock** board (`gen_dock.py`, `hw/dock/`).
   - NINA pin 31 (VBUS) was left open in rev 0.7, with **R34** (0R to GND, DNP). **Rev 0.8 fits R34** (section 2e).
   - C29 10 uF stays as bulk on +BATT. Q2 stays.
   - With no USB, firmware is flashed with a cell fitted (the programmer does not power the pod).
2. **Two-pod clock link.**
   - ADS1298 CLKSEL moves from DVDD to NINA pin 23 (P0.29), with **R33 100k** to +3V3: internal oscillator by
     default, firmware pulls it low on a slave pod.
   - ADS CLK (net CLK_SYNC, also on TP1) -> **R31 100R** -> pogo **P11** (net CLK_POGO), ESD on D4's spare IO3.
     **R32 1M** to GND, so CLK never floats.
   - The master sets CONFIG1 CLK_EN = 1. Without a lead, each pod time-stamps its samples and the phone aligns the two streams.
   - START needs no pin: both pods start on a BLE command, and the fixed whole-sample offset is removed from the
     timestamps.
3. **MAX17048 fuel gauge U7** (TDFN-8 2 x 2):
   - VDD and CELL on +BATT; CTG, QSTRT and EP on GND; ALRT open (polled); C35 100 nF (datasheet: bypass VDD with 0.1 uF).
   - SDA = EXP4 (pin 21), SCL = EXP5 (pin 22), R35/R36 4.7k to +3V3. The bus stays on the expansion pads, so add-on
     sensors share it.
   - These pins are near the radio. I2C runs about 1 ms once a minute, timed between BLE events (reviewer question).
   - The 1M/1M divider stays as a fallback.
   - Pin table checked 2026-10-17 against datasheet 19-6171 p. 6: identical.
4. **Board size stays 24 x 30.** 24 x 28 was checked first, on paper: cell 20 mm + H1 screw-head clearance + the
   4.3 mm cartridge-pad strip = 28.0 mm with zero margin, and the case needs about ±0.4 mm for the cartridge.
5. **Board 1.2 mm thick** (2026-10-17): JLCPCB offers 6 layers at 1.2 mm for the same price, with the same rules
   (JLCPCB capabilities and quote pages, checked 2026-10-17). Spring-pin heights are measured from the board faces, so nothing else moves.
   The pod gets 0.4 mm thinner. Short top-side pins were checked and rejected: thinner gold, and no gain with a
   1.2 mm PETG wall.
6. **Layout:** the charger's strip now holds U7, C35, R35/R36 and, right under P11, R31/R32. R33 and R34 take the
   free slots in the right-edge resistor columns.
   - ADS GND pin 20 lost its via-reachable pour (an island under the module), so `fix_gnd_pin.py U1 20` routes it to
     the nearest GND via, as pin 51 already was.
   - CLK_SYNC is about 37 mm of track (ADS CLK pin -> TP1 -> R31/R32). It only toggles on a master pod with CLK_EN
     set (reviewer question).
7. **Electrode tab:**
   - The 2x5 electrode header J1 is unchanged.
   - A new SMD 1x2 header J2 above the grid carries CLK and GND, for a 2-wire lead between two pods' tabs.
   - A single 2x6 header was tried and did not route on one layer.

## 2e. Rev 0.8 changes (2026-10-19)

1. **VCAP1 cap C14 is now a polymer tantalum:** Vishay T58MM226M6R3C0300 (LCSC **C215879**), 22 µF, 6.3 V, 1608-09 case,
   0.9 mm max height, 300 mΩ max ESR, about $0.57, JLCPCB Extended, in stock. Backup: KEMET T597S226M010APE100
   (C19941081, 3216-12, 1.2 mm, but only 40 in stock). Research: `research/jaw/tantalum.md`.
   - **Why:** SBAS459K p. 96: where the board sees vibration, use a nonferroelectric VCAP1 cap, "a tantalum or class 1
     capacitor (C0G or NPO)"; X5R is piezoelectric and picks up microphonics. The same page says noise on VCAP1 degrades
     performance (forum answer Q3, `research/jaw/chip-forums.md`).
   - **Voltage:** with the 3.3 V analog supply the reference is 2.4 V. The 4 V figure in the research file (datasheet p. 33)
     is the 5 V-supply option (VREF_4V), which this board does not use. 6.3 V clears either.
   - **Footprint:** `Capacitor_Tantalum_SMD:CP_EIA-1608-10_AVX-L`, with the Vishay T58 pad table giving the same pads for the
     MM (1608-09) and M0 (1608-10) cases. **Pad 1 = +**, on VCAP1 (toward ADS pin 28); pad 2 = GND. Symbol Device:C_Polarized.
     Placed where the 0805 was (bottom side, second row). Checked in the rebuilt PCB and in `check_nets.py`.
   - **Leakage:** polymer, up to 14 µA at 6.3 V (about 0.1·C·V in general terms). The ADS1298 gives no leakage limit for VCAP1
     (reviewer question 6).
2. **NINA pin 31 (VBUS) is tied to GND through R34 0R, now fitted** (was open, DNP). Nordic's no-USB reference configuration
   and the DevZone threads 35658 and 48589 say to ground VBUS (forum answer Q5, `research/jaw/chip-forums.md`). The
   reviewer question about it stays as a confirmation.
3. Nothing else changed. Pod board 24 × 30 mm, 91 placed parts, DRC 0, unconnected 0, parity 0, ERC 0, net rules pass.

## 5. Firmware rules the hardware depends on

- **Power-up:** drive PWDN and RESET high, wait for t_POR, pulse RESET, send SDATAC, and only then
  write registers (SBAS459K power-up sequence).
- **ADS1298 registers:**
  - CONFIG3: `PD_REFBUF = 1` (internal reference), `RLDREF_INT = 1`, `PD_RLD = 1`.
  - RLD_SENSP/N: select the electrode channels.
  - Gain: x6 or lower for EMG (the forum found x24 rails).
  - Data rate: 1000 SPS by default (owner 2026-10-07); 500 only as the weak-link fallback.
  - Do not use the pace/test outputs. They are tied to AVDD.
- **Charging (rev 0.7):** never on the pod. A cartridge swap restarts the pod; buffer to flash and back-fill after the
  BLE reconnect. Battery % comes from the MAX17048 (fallback: BAT_SENSE).
- **Two pods:** the master keeps CLKSEL high and sets CONFIG1 CLK_EN = 1; the slave drives CLKSEL low before START.
  Only one pod drives BIAS (the other powers its RLD amplifier down).
- **No light anywhere:** there are no LEDs. Every status (connected, recording, battery, charging)
  goes to the phone app over BLE.
- **Battery:** read BAT_SENSE × 2. Below 3.5 V, warn and stop, because LDO dropout starts near 3.4 V.
- **Idle:** keep PWDN low and advertise BLE slowly. There is no power switch, so idle current sets
  shelf life (tens of µA is weeks to months on the 120 mAh cartridge; MAX17048 adds about 23 µA, or 4 µA in hibernate).

## 6. Layout (rev 0.4 board, 2026-10-14; rev 0.5-0.7 changes in sections 2c and 2d)

Rebuild everything with `hw/pod/build.sh`:

| Step | Script | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `gen_sch.py` | schematic, ERC, netlist, `check_nets.py` |
| 2 | `gen_nina_fp.py` | module footprint |
| 3 | `gen_pcb.py` | placement, rules, planes, keep-outs, locked decoupling routes, GND fan-out |
| 4 | `route.py` | Freerouting |
| 5 | `finish_route.py` | A* for leftovers, GND stitching, clearance nudges |
| 6 | DRC | must be clean |

Order files come from `fab.py`, which refuses on any DRC error, unrouted net or placeholder. The mating
tabs come from `gen_tabs.py`, and the electrode tab is then routed by `route_tab.py` (F.Cu only). Shared mechanics are in `pod_geom.py`. The review package is `hw/review/`.

- **Board:** 24 x 30 mm, **6 layers**: F.Cu / In1 GND / In2 sig / In3 sig / In4 GND / B.Cu, parts on both
  sides. The owner chose 24 x 30 after 22 x 28 measured 76 % courtyard density (893 mm2 of parts). Six
  layers instead of four (about $10-30 more per order) because:
  - on four layers the autorouter left 8-27 nets open in every attempt;
  - an In2 +3V3 plane split into islands;
  - six layers give each assembled side its own solid ground plane.
  Rev 0.6+: the 402020 cell (20 x 20 x 4, about 120 mAh) sits on the removable cartridge against the bottom side.
- **Top:**
  - NINA-B306 at the top edge, antenna outward (footprint rotated 180 deg), with an all-layer copper
    keep-out under its antenna end;
  - 3 x 4 pogo grid (3.0 mm pitch, P1-P3 / P4-P6 / P7-P9 / P10-P12; rev 0.7: P11 CLK_POGO + P12 GND), with
    an M2 NPTH clamp hole at each end;
  - Tag-Connect between H1 and the grid;
  - rev 0.7: R31 (CLK 100R) and R32 (1M) under P11, where the VBUS TVS was;
  - flash directly under the module's QSPI pin row;
  - rev 0.7: MAX17048 + pull-ups where the charger was; FET, JST SH socket (opening at the bottom edge);
  - the boot pull resistors and battery divider in two columns at the right edge.
- **Bottom:**
  - ADS1298 under the module;
  - every decoupling cap 2 mm outside its own pin, with a **locked** pad-to-pin route;
  - VREFP 10 uF and VCAP1 22 uF (rev 0.8: 1608 polymer tantalum) in a second row, lined up on free gaps;
  - the 10 filter rows (bead, 2.2k, 1 nF) under the pogo grid in ADS pin order, ESD arrays in the
    grid-area corners;
  - both LDOs, with EN tied to IN on a locked route;
  - 0.8 mm test and expansion pads.
- **Clamp:** the bench electrode tab screws down on 3.0 mm M2 nylon spacers (pogo
  working height 2.9 mm). Hole keep-out is r 2.1 mm on both sides (spacer on top, screw head below).
  Screws are for the bench tab only. The worn flex strip, the battery cartridge and the dock hold on with magnets in
  the printed case (owner, 2026-10-06/07). The two NPTH holes double as locating posts.
- **Tabs:**
  - the pod-facing side carries only the contact pads, so a pogo tip can't press through the mask onto
    other copper;
  - the electrode tab stays inside the pod outline (rev 0.5); the charger tab is gone (rev 0.7);
  - electrode tab header J1 (upright SMD 2x5, pin 1 top left, odd pins left column), rev 0.7 order: 1 REF, 2 E6,
    3 E3, 4 E5, 5 E4, 6 E2, 7 E1, 8 E8, 9 BIAS, 10 E7. Link header J2 (1x2): 1 CLK, 2 GND. The order is the one that routes on the top layer alone; the
    silkscreen prints it beside the header.
- **Rules (JLCPCB 6-layer standard):**
  - signals routed at 0.10 / 0.10 mm; necked tracks widened to the 0.09 mm minimum;
  - DRC uses 0.09 mm space;
  - vias 0.45 / 0.20 mm;
  - via hole to copper 0.20 mm (confirm in review);
  - copper to edge 0.30 mm;
  - battery path (+BATT, BAT_RAW) 0.20 mm tracks.
- **Ground:** every GND SMD pad gets its own stub and via where one fits. The pads listed in the review
  README reach ground through the pour plus stitching vias, and DRC confirms them connected.
