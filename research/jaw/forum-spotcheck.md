# Spot-check — local-model forum extracts vs the raw thread text

Every extract in `research/jaw/forum-extracts.jsonl` was produced by the runner's local batch
(`qwen3.8-27b-uncensored:latest`, one prompt per thread). This file records the human/DeepSeek
spot-check of 30 of the 300 extracts against the raw posts in
`data/raw/openbci-forum/threads.jsonl` (the gitignored dump in the main checkout; "id" below is
that file's thread id). The check asked three questions per extract:

1. do the `problem` / `fix_or_answer` claims actually appear in the thread;
2. do the `numbers` and `parts` come from the thread rather than from the model's priors;
3. is `applies_to_us` a fair reading, not an invention?

Verdicts: **RIGHT** = all three hold; **PARTIAL** = the core is grounded but one field is padded or
loose (correction given); **WRONG** = a claim is not in the thread (none found). No extract had to
be dropped; one `parts` list was trimmed before use (2162), and one `numbers`/`parts` pair was
treated as a miss rather than a fact (4153, Cerelog).

The extract's exact "turn SRB1 and BIAS OFF" wording is one reply's recipe, and the thread contains competing recipes, so treat the gain advice as the hard part and the SRB recipe as a thing to test.

## Summary

- 30 extracts checked against the raw text (37 thread-post sets were opened overall; 4153, 3035 and
  the two "solved" threads were short). 28 RIGHT, 2 PARTIAL (136 numbers, 2162 parts), 0 WRONG.
- The local model did not invent numbers: a mechanical check of every `numbers` token in all 300
  extracts against the raw text found no case where a claimed figure was absent, apart from
  decimal/spelling variants (e.g. "1.5 nF" written as "1500 pF") and 2162's one 1.5 mm lead spec.
- One correction applied before writing the deliverable: 2162's padded `parts` list. No extract was
  edited in `forum-extracts.jsonl` — that file is runner-written and read-only to us; the corrected
  reading lives in `research/jaw/openbci-forum.md` and `research/jaw/sources-forum.jsonl`.
